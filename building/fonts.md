# Fonts

- **Check the license.** "Free for personal use" fonts can't go on a business site. (favoritesonnyc, jasiahpowers)
- **Never rely on an OS-only font.** Use a stack: the OS font, then a self-hosted lookalike, then a generic. Why: non-Apple visitors saw Courier New. (favoritesonnyc)
- **After a font change, confirm the new font actually loads live,** including on a non-Apple device. Why: an old font kept showing after a "swap." (jasiahpowers)
