# Legal baseline

Applies to every new client site. No need to retrofit past projects.

- **Every new client site ships a privacy policy, an accessibility statement and a consent line under each form.** Why: Abis had none at first, and it wasn't in the brief. (Abis #49)
- **No third-party "auto-ADA" accessibility overlay widgets** (accessiBe, UserWay and the like). Fix the site itself, and have the statement describe what the site actually does and how to report a problem. This is not about cookie banners. Why: overlays don't fix the site, and they add a third-party script.
- **Restaurants add an allergy line and the raw or undercooked advisory,** with a per-dish flag in the CMS that puts the asterisk on the dish and the footnote on the menu. (Abis #49)
- **Add a cookie consent banner whenever a tracker or ad pixel is added; without one, skip it.** Why: Abis sets no cookies (Cloudflare Web Analytics is cookieless), so it needs no banner. (Abis)
- **Legal pages are plain copy Jake approves,** not legal advice. Mark anything that needs the client's facts `[TBD: Jake to confirm]`. → [design.md](design.md)
