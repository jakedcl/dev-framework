# Design and content

dcldev aims to look like a real, craft-first studio.

- **Real copy only.** Never invent facts or results, and mark unknowns `[TBD: Jake to confirm]`. (dcldev studio)
- **Blank beats wrong.** Don't publish unconfirmed or scraped content. (bannquet, Abis)
- **Keep the existing look, fonts and buttons unless asked.** Use no new UI kits for small changes. Why: four sites got "undo" or "I hate it." (bannquet, compactpickup, metrotapes, jasiahpowers)
- **Never redraw a client's logo.** Trace it once and only restyle it, and the favicon uses the real logo. (favoritesonnyc, bannquet)
- **Restyles follow one reference page,** and shared components (buttons, navs, tables) have fixed sizes. Why: mismatched buttons kept coming back. (logiparty, Abis, bannquet)
- **Don't look vibecoded:** polished but personal, with no AI tells (purple gradients, glow blobs, templated buttons, walls of text). (jasiahpowers, Abis)
- **Write for the client's customers, not developers.** No tutorial copy. (Abis, bannquet)
- **Check every asset at real size, and use all the good client photos** (say which went unused). Why: a "logo" turned out to be a printer spec sheet. (Abis)
- **Never render empty containers, and keep padding symmetric.** (Abis)
- **Clickable things look like buttons.** (compactpickup)
- **Motion is one shared, subtle system:** same timing everywhere, quick moves, nothing popping in. (jasiahpowers, Abis, metrotapes)
