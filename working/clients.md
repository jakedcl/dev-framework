# Clients and agency

Client work runs as dcldev (dcldev.com, jake@dcldev.com).

- **Client info lives in Notion Clients & Projects, and code knowledge in each repo's AGENTS.md.** Why: anyone can pick a project back up. (cursor helper)
- **The client owns their accounts** (hosting, domain, email). At handoff the client is Super Administrator and billing owner with 2FA on, Jake drops to Administrator, and agent and connector access is narrowed by default; the exact scope is decided per client. (Abis)
- **Nothing goes to a client until Jake says so.** (Abis)
- **Client-visible placeholders sound like Jake, never like an agent.** Keep the full to-do list in a private launch checklist. Why: "he thinks i made this." (Abis)
- **The first message to a client lists unfinished items honestly.** (Abis)
- **Write a handoff before retiring a helper, and handoff docs go to Jake, not the repo.** (metrotapes, jasiahpowers)
- **The client handoff pack is a standard deliverable:** account transfer (above) and a plain-language editing guide with screenshots, including how to use Presentation. Like other handoff docs, it goes to Jake first. (Abis)
- **The handoff doc has a Search Console section:** it's set up, Jake (jake@dcldev.com) is the verified owner and the client is an Owner, never delete the `google-site-verification` TXT record in DNS, and search traffic is under Performance > Search results (the Queries tab shows what people searched). Why: a deleted record cuts off the client's access too. (Jake, Oct 30, 2026)
- **Keep one running list of questions for the client and send it as one batch.** Nothing should wait on it. Why: Abis's questions piled up across chats until they became one 24-item file. (Abis)
- **Clients edit from their phones, so keep their CMS simple.** (731photography)
- **Basic SEO is part of every build,** and the client adds jake@dcldev.com as a Business Profile Manager, not Owner. (Abis)
- **Get the client's OK before featuring their site.** (dcldev studio)
