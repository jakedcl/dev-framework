- [ ] P3, business sites: Search Console verified with the client's own TXT record, jake@dcldev.com added as a Full user, and the sitemap submitted. → [seo-launch.md](../hosting/seo-launch.md)
- [ ] The handoff doc says where to see searches (Performance > Search results > Queries) and to leave the `google-site-verification` TXT record in DNS. → [seo-launch.md](../hosting/seo-launch.md)
